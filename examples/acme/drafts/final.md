### What you get on Pro and Crew

- **Texts.** Text a customer from the job card.
- **Calls.** The Acme app logs each call you place on the job card.
- **Emails.** An email sent to a job's address appears on that job card.
- **Your books.** Acme sends 6 fields to LedgerLite. The import is one way.

### Plans and trial

Starter, Pro and Crew cost $29, $79 and $149 a month. You can try Acme free for 14 days.

Call recording rules differ by state. Check yours before you record a call.

## how-made

**How this kit was made**

1. I filled the coverage table first. It has 5 source types.
2. I opened 41 of 41 help center articles, 12 of 12 release notes and 9 of 9 webinar recordings. I read 3 of 8 partner documentation pages. Facts about the partners rest on those 3 pages.
3. I put each product fact in the facts table with a label. Another model family tried to overturn the confirmed and absent rows. None came back overturned.
4. I counted 335 complaints in public reviews of one competing scheduling product. The count is close, not exact. The mobile app was the third-largest complaint, and support and billing made up 64% of all complaints.
5. I did not run the page check, because no page was built. I did not run the panel check or the release hash check. No reader outside the kit has seen this draft.
